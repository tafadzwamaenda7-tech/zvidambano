Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 1 July 2026

1. Introduction

ZVIDAMBANO Traders (Private) Limited ("ZVIDAMBANO", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, use our services, or otherwise engage with us in the course of our agricultural trading operations.

By accessing or using our website and services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access or use our website or services.

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Zimbabwe, including the Data Protection Act [Chapter 3:33] and any subsidiary legislation made thereunder. We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

We collect various categories of information in connection with your use of our website and services. The types of information we collect include:

Personal Information

Personal information is data that can be used to identify you directly or indirectly. We may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name, telephone number, and email address
  • Physical address and postal address
  • National identification number or passport number
  • Business registration details, including company name and registration number
  • Bank account details and financial information necessary for transaction processing
  • Payment and billing information
  • Professional or occupational information, including employer and job title

Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our website or interact with our online platforms:

  • Pages visited and content viewed on our website
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source of traffic
  • Search terms used to find our website
  • Actions taken on the website, including form submissions and downloads

Device and Browser Data

We collect information about the device and browser you use to access our services:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and platform
  • Device type, model, and screen resolution
  • Language settings and time zone

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information through the following methods:

  • Directly from you: When you fill in forms on our website, create an account, request a quotation, subscribe to newsletters, contact us by email or telephone, or provide information during the course of a business relationship.
  • Automatically: When you browse our website, we use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect usage data and device information. For further details, see Section 9 (Cookies and Tracking Technologies).
  • From third parties: We may receive information about you from business partners, agricultural cooperatives, out-grower networks, credit reference agencies, publicly available databases, or social media platforms where you have made information available.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and manage our services: Facilitating the buying, selling, processing, and transportation of agricultural commodities, managing out-grower programmes, and delivering training services.
  • To process transactions: Processing payments, issuing invoices, managing accounts, and fulfilling contractual obligations arising from your engagement with ZVIDAMBANO.
  • To communicate with you: Responding to enquiries, providing customer support, sending service-related notices, and delivering marketing communications where you have consented to receive them.
  • To improve our platform: Analysing website usage patterns, conducting research and analytics, developing new features, and enhancing the user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations: Meeting regulatory requirements under Zimbabwean law, responding to lawful requests from governmental authorities, and fulfilling reporting obligations applicable to agricultural trading.
  • To detect and prevent fraud: Monitoring for suspicious activity, protecting against unauthorised access, and maintaining the security and integrity of our systems.
  • To manage business relationships: Maintaining records of out-grower agreements, supplier contracts, and client engagements in the ordinary course of business.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with the following categories of recipients, and only to the extent necessary for the stated purposes:

  • Service providers: Third-party vendors who perform functions on our behalf, including payment processors, IT infrastructure providers, cloud hosting services, logistics and transport companies, and marketing platforms. These providers are contractually bound to use your information only for the purposes for which it was disclosed and to maintain appropriate security measures.
  • Business partners: Agricultural cooperatives, out-grower programme partners, buyers, and other entities with whom we collaborate in the ordinary course of our agricultural trading operations.
  • Legal authorities: Courts, regulatory bodies, law enforcement agencies, or other governmental authorities where disclosure is required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ZVIDAMBANO, our clients, or the public.
  • Mergers and acquisitions: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, sale of assets, or bankruptcy, your personal information may be transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you of any such change and any choices you may have regarding your information.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocols
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest where appropriate
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular backups and disaster recovery procedures
  • Employee training on data protection and information security

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data, but we are committed to promptly notifying you and the relevant authorities of any data breach that may affect your personal information, in accordance with Zimbabwean data protection legislation.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting requirements.

In determining the appropriate retention period, we consider the nature and sensitivity of the data, the potential risk of harm from unauthorised use or disclosure, the purposes for which we process the data, and applicable legal requirements. Specifically:

  • Transaction records and financial data are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years, in compliance with Zimbabwean tax and financial regulations.
  • Contractual records related to out-grower agreements and supplier arrangements are retained for the duration of the agreement and for six (6) years thereafter, in accordance with the Prescription Act [Chapter 8:11].
  • Marketing consent records are retained for as long as consent remains active and for twelve (12) months after withdrawal of consent.
  • Website analytics data is retained in aggregated, anonymised form for as long as reasonably necessary for analytical purposes.

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised so that it can no longer be associated with you.

8. Your Rights

Subject to Zimbabwean law, you have the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Right of access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy of that information.
  • Right of correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information we hold about you.
  • Right of deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
  • Right to opt out of marketing: You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link provided in our emails or by contacting us directly. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the opt-out.
  • Right to data portability: Where technically feasible, you may request a copy of your personal information in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where we rely on your consent to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 14. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to distinguish you from other users, improve your browsing experience, and analyse website traffic. Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Essential for the operation of our website. Without these cookies, services you have requested cannot be provided.
  • Performance and analytics cookies: Collect information about how visitors use our website, including which pages are visited most often. This information is used to improve the website's functionality.
  • Functionality cookies: Remember choices you make to provide enhanced, personalised features, such as language preferences.
  • Marketing cookies: Track your browsing habits to deliver advertisements relevant to your interests. These are only set with your explicit consent.

You may manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For further information on managing cookies, consult your browser's help documentation.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services that are not operated or controlled by ZVIDAMBANO. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of any third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit. The inclusion of a link on our website does not imply endorsement of the linked site or any association with its operators.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of eighteen (18). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate parental or guardian consent, we will take steps to delete that information as soon as reasonably possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us immediately using the details in Section 14.

12. International Data Transfers

ZVIDAMBANO is based in Zimbabwe, and your personal information is primarily processed within Zimbabwe. However, certain service providers or business partners may be located in other countries, which may involve the transfer of your data outside Zimbabwe.

Where we transfer your personal information outside Zimbabwe, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data, including:

  • Ensuring the recipient country has adequate data protection laws, as determined by the relevant Zimbabwean data protection authority
  • Entering into standard contractual clauses or other appropriate data transfer mechanisms with the recipient
  • Ensuring the recipient adheres to binding corporate rules or other recognised frameworks for data protection

By using our services, you acknowledge and consent to the transfer of your personal information as described in this section.

13. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Where changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, such as by posting a notice on our website or contacting you directly via email.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about how we are protecting your information. Your continued use of our website and services after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms.
