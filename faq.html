Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading on the ZVIDAMBANO platform.

Visit our platform and click "Get Started" to create your account. You'll need to provide your name, contact details, and business information. Once submitted, our team will review and verify your account within 2-3 business days. You'll receive an email confirmation once you're approved and ready to start trading.

ZVIDAMBANO supports a wide range of agricultural commodities including grains (maize, wheat, rice, barley), legumes (soya beans, sugar beans, ground nuts), fresh produce (tomatoes, potatoes, vegetables), livestock products (cattle, poultry, eggs), stockfeed, farm inputs, and agricultural equipment. Our catalog is constantly growing as more suppliers join the platform.

All transactions on ZVIDAMBANO are conducted in USD. Once you place an order and it's confirmed by the supplier, payment is held in escrow until delivery is verified. We support bank transfers and mobile money payments. Funds are released to the supplier only after you confirm receipt of goods in the expected condition and quantity.

Yes. Every supplier on our platform goes through a verification process. Products are graded according to industry standards, and our field officers conduct quality checks at procurement depots. For grains and cereals, we follow Zimbabwean grading standards. Each listing includes grade information, origin, and available quantity so you can make informed purchasing decisions.

ZVIDAMBANO has an integrated logistics network. When you place an order, you can opt for ZVIDAMBANO-managed delivery from one of our 10 procurement depots across Zimbabwe. Alternatively, you can arrange your own collection. For large volume orders, we coordinate transport through our verified transporter partners to ensure safe and timely delivery.

Yes. ZVIDAMBANO is developing financing solutions for farmers and trade customers. Farmers in our out-grower program can access input financing (seeds, fertilizer, equipment) with guaranteed offtake agreements. Trade customers can access finance for bulk purchases. Contact our team to learn more about current financing options and eligibility requirements.

We currently operate 10 procurement depots across all provinces of Zimbabwe ? Harare, Chinhoyi, Marondera, Bindura, Gweru, Masvingo, Mutare, Bulawayo, Kwekwe, and Chiredzi. We have also expanded into Zambia with an office in Lusaka. ZVIDA buys and sells nationwide, with logistics support for inter-regional trade.

Account verification typically takes 2-3 business days. During this period, our team reviews your submitted documents and business details. You may be contacted for additional information. Once verified, you'll receive full access to the platform including selling your products to ZVIDA, buying through ZVIDA, and accessing logistics and financing services.
